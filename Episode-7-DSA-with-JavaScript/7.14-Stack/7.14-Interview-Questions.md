## Beginner

### Q1. Stack order?

**Answer:**
LIFO — Last In, First Out

## Beginner

### Q2. Push/Pop time?

**Answer:**
O(1)

---

## Beginner

### Q3. Valid parentheses?

**Answer:**
Push open, pop and compare close

---

## Beginner

### Q4. Next greater element?

**Answer:**
Stack from right, pop smaller

## Beginner

### Q5. Min stack?

**Answer:**
Auxiliary stack tracking minimums

## Intermediate

### Q6. Stack vs Queue?

**Answer:**
Stack: LIFO, Queue: FIFO

## Intermediate

### Q7. Postfix evaluation?

## Intermediate

### Q8. Call stack?

**Answer:**
Function calls use stack (LIFO)

## Intermediate

### Q9. Largest rect histogram?

**Answer:**
Stack of indices, calculate on pop

### Q10. Monotonic stack?

**Answer:**
Stack maintaining increasing/decreasing order
