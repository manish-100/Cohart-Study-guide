### Q4. Diameter?

**Answer:**

## Beginner

### Q5. LCA approach?

**Answer:**

### Q6. Balanced tree?

**Answer:**
|left height - right height| <= 1 for all nodes

## Intermediate

### Q7. Full vs Complete?
