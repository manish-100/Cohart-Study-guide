### Q1. Which of the following is a leap year?

(a) 1900
(b) 2100
(c) 2400
(d) 1800

**Answer: (c) 2400**
```
All are century years. Only 2400 is divisible by 400.
2400/400 = 6 (exact) -> Leap year
```
