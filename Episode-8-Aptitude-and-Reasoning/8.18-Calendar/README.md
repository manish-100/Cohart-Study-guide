# 8.18 Calendar

## Overview

**Calendar** problems are a staple of aptitude exams. They test your ability to determine the day of the week for any given date, understand leap year rules, and work with the concept of "odd days." These problems appear frequently in banking exams, SSC, railway exams, and campus placements.

## Why This Topic Matters

- **Exam frequency:** 1-3 questions in most competitive exams
- **Quick scoring:** With the odd days method mastered, problems are solvable in 15-30 seconds
- **No calculator needed:** Pure logic and memorization-based topic

## Prerequisites

- Division and remainder concepts
- Understanding of leap years
- Basic modular arithmetic (helpful but not required)
