# 8.21 Blood Relation

## Overview

Blood Relation problems test your ability to determine the relationship between members of a family based on given information. These are among the most frequently asked reasoning questions across all competitive exams.

## What You Will Learn

1. **Relationship Terms** -- Paternal, maternal, in-law, generation levels
2. **Family Tree Construction** -- Building diagrams from verbal descriptions
3. **Coded Relationships** -- Decoding symbols (+ - * / etc.) for relations
4. **Pointing-Based Problems** -- "Pointing to a photograph" type questions
5. **Common Relationship Phrases** -- Decoding tricky English expressions
