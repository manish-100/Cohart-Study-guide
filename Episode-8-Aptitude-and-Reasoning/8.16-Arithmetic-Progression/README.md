# 8.16 Arithmetic Progression (AP)

## Overview

An **Arithmetic Progression** is one of the most fundamental topics in quantitative aptitude. It deals with sequences where the difference between consecutive terms remains constant. AP concepts are widely tested in competitive exams, banking exams, SSC, CAT, GRE, GMAT, and campus placement tests.

## Why This Topic Matters

- **Exam frequency:** 2-4 questions appear in most competitive exams
- **Foundation topic:** AP concepts are used in time-series analysis, financial calculations (EMI, depreciation), and physics
- **Scoring:** Once formulas are memorized, AP problems can be solved in under 30 seconds

## Prerequisites

- Basic algebra (linear equations)
- Understanding of sequences and series
- Summation concepts
