# 8.23 Arrangements (Seating / Linear / Circular)

## Overview

Arrangement problems test your ability to determine the positions of people or objects based on given conditions. These are among the most scoring and most frequently asked topics in Banking, SSC, and management entrance exams. They require systematic logic and careful tracking of constraints.

## What You Will Learn

1. **Linear Arrangement** -- People sitting in a row (one or two rows)
2. **Circular Arrangement** -- People sitting around a table
3. **Constraint Types** -- Adjacent, not adjacent, between, at ends, facing direction
4. **Solving Approach** -- Fix, arrange, check method
5. **Complex Arrangements** -- Double rows, facing each other, with additional attributes
