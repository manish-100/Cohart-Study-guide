**Instructions:** Choose the best answer. Some questions are standalone; others form a set based on a common puzzle. Answers and explanations are at the end.

## Set 1 (Q1-Q5): Linear Arrangement

**Six friends P, Q, R, S, T, U sit in a row facing North.**
- R sits at the right end.
- P is 3rd from the left.
- Q sits to the immediate left of R.
- T is not adjacent to P.
- U sits at the left end.

### Q1.
Who sits in the middle of the row (positions 3 and 4)?

(a) P and S  
(b) P and Q  
(c) P and T  
(d) S and T  

### Q2.
Who is to the immediate right of P?

(a) T  
(b) S  
(c) Q  
(d) Cannot be determined  

### Q3.
How many people sit between U and Q?

(a) 2  
(b) 3  
(c) 4  
(d) 1  

### Q4.
Who sits at position 4 from the left?

(a) S  
(b) T  
(c) Q  
(d) Cannot be determined  

### Q5.
If T and S interchange positions, who would be adjacent to Q?

(a) S and R  
(b) T and R  
(c) P and R  
(d) Cannot be determined

### Q44. (a) Clockwise

### Q45. (a) 1st
