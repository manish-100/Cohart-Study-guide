# 8.11 Speed, Distance, and Time

## Overview

Speed, Distance, and Time is one of the most fundamental and frequently tested topics in
aptitude examinations. Mastering this section builds a strong foundation for related topics
like Problems on Trains, Boats and Streams, and Races.

## What You Will Learn

- The core relationship: Speed = Distance / Time
- Unit conversions between km/h and m/s
- Relative speed concepts (same and opposite direction)
- Average speed for multi-leg journeys
- Meeting and overtaking problems
- Circular track problems
