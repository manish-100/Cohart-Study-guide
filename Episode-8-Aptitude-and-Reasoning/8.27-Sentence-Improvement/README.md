# Section 8.27 -- Sentence Improvement

## Overview

Sentence Improvement is a key topic in the Verbal Reasoning / English Language section of competitive exams. You are given a sentence with a **bold or underlined part** and must choose the best replacement from the given options. If the sentence is already correct, the answer is "No improvement" or "No change needed."

Unlike Error Identification (which asks "where is the error?"), Sentence Improvement asks "what is the best way to say this?" -- requiring you to evaluate clarity, grammar, conciseness, and idiom usage.
