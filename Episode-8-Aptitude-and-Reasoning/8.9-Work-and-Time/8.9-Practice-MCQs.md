### Q21.
A takes 6 days less than B to do a certain job. Together they can do the job in 4 days. How many days does A take?

(a) 6
(b) 8
(c) 10
(d) 12

<details>
<summary>Answer and Explanation</summary>

**Answer: (a) 6**

```
Let B = x days, A = (x-6) days.

Together: (x)(x-6) / (x + x - 6) = 4

x(x-6) / (2x-6) = 4
x^2 - 6x = 8x - 24
x^2 - 14x + 24 = 0
(x - 12)(x - 2) = 0
x = 12 or x = 2

x = 2 makes A = -4 (invalid). So x = 12.

A = 12 - 6 = 6 days
B = 12 days

Check: (6 x 12)/(6+12) = 72/18 = 4 days. Correct!
```
</details>

### Q42.
Two workers A and B are engaged to do a piece of work. A working alone takes 8 hours more than A and B together. B working alone takes 18 hours more than A and B together. How long would they take working together?

(a) 6 hours
(b) 10 hours
(c) 12 hours
(d) 15 hours

<details>
<summary>Answer and Explanation</summary>

**Answer: (c) 12 hours**

```
Let time together = T hours.
A alone = T + 8
B alone = T + 18

Using the formula: 1/(T+8) + 1/(T+18) = 1/T

Multiply through by T(T+8)(T+18):
   T(T+18) + T(T+8) = (T+8)(T+18)
   T^2 + 18T + T^2 + 8T = T^2 + 26T + 144
   2T^2 + 26T = T^2 + 26T + 144
   T^2 = 144
   T = 12

Shortcut: For this type of problem:
   T = sqrt(extra_A x extra_B) = sqrt(8 x 18) = sqrt(144) = 12

Answer: 12 hours
```

**Important Shortcut:** When "A alone takes 'a' hours more than together" and "B alone takes 'b' hours more than together," time together = sqrt(a x b).
</details>
