# 8.22 Syllogism

## Overview

Syllogism is a form of logical reasoning where a conclusion is drawn from two or more given premises (statements). This topic is a staple in competitive exams, especially Banking, SSC, and CAT. The Venn diagram method is the most reliable approach to solve these problems.

## What You Will Learn

1. **Types of Statements** -- All, Some, No, Some-Not
2. **Venn Diagram Method** -- Drawing and testing all possible diagrams
3. **Universal & Particular Statements** -- Affirmative and Negative
4. **Valid Conclusions** -- What can and cannot be concluded
5. **Either-Or Cases** -- When exactly one of two complementary conclusions must hold
6. **Multiple Premises** -- Handling three or more statements
