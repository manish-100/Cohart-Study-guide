# 8.24 Series (Number Series / Sequence)

## Overview

Series problems test your ability to recognize patterns in sequences of numbers, letters, or figures. Number series is one of the most frequently asked topics across all competitive exams and placement tests. Speed and pattern recognition are key to mastering this topic.

## What You Will Learn

1. **Arithmetic Series** -- Constant difference patterns
2. **Geometric Series** -- Constant ratio patterns
3. **Difference Series** -- Patterns in differences of consecutive terms
4. **Product/Division Series** -- Multiplicative patterns
5. **Alternate/Mixed Series** -- Multiple interleaved patterns
6. **Special Series** -- Fibonacci-like, prime-based, square/cube-based
7. **Wrong Number Detection** -- Find the incorrect term
8. **Missing Number** -- Find the next or missing term
