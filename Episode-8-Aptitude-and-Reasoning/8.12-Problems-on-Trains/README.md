# 8.12 Problems on Trains

## Overview

Problems on Trains is a specialized application of Speed, Distance, and Time concepts.
The key difference is that trains have a physical length that must be accounted for when
calculating crossing times. This topic is a staple of aptitude exams across all levels.

## What You Will Learn

- Train crossing a stationary object (pole, person, signal)
- Train crossing a platform or bridge (objects with length)
- Train crossing another train (same and opposite direction)
- Relative speed with moving observers (man on platform vs man on train)
- Combined problems with multiple conditions
