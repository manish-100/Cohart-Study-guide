# 8.19 Clocks

## Overview

**Clock** problems test your understanding of the angular movement of clock hands, the relative speed between the minute and hour hands, and time-related calculations. This is a high-scoring topic in aptitude exams because the formulas are few but the applications are varied.

## Why This Topic Matters

- **Exam frequency:** 1-3 questions in banking, SSC, and placement exams
- **Formula-driven:** Only 2-3 core formulas cover 90% of problems
- **Tricky variations:** Mirror images and faulty clocks add challenge

## Prerequisites

- Understanding of angles and degrees
- Basic speed, distance, and time concepts
- Familiarity with fractions
