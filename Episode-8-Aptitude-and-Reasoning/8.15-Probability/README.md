# 8.15 Probability

## Overview

Probability is one of the most universally tested topics across all competitive exams. It quantifies the likelihood of events and builds directly on the concepts of Permutations and Combinations (Section 8.14). This section covers everything from the basic definition of probability through conditional probability, with extensive practice on dice, cards, coins, and real-world scenarios.

## What You Will Learn

- **Definition of Probability** -- P(E) = Favourable outcomes / Total outcomes
- **Sample Space and Events** -- understanding the universe of outcomes
- **Complementary Probability** -- P(not E) = 1 - P(E)
- **Addition Rule** -- for mutually exclusive and non-mutually exclusive events
- **Multiplication Rule** -- for independent and dependent events
- **Independent vs Dependent Events** -- critical distinction
- **Conditional Probability** -- P(A|B) and its applications
- **Dice Problems** -- single die, two dice, sum-based problems
- **Card Problems** -- standard 52-card deck selections
- **Coin Problems** -- single/multiple tosses, at-least problems

## Study Tips

1. **Always define the sample space first** -- this prevents most errors.
2. For "at least" problems, prefer complementary probability.
3. Draw tree diagrams for multi-stage experiments.
4. Memorize the outcomes for 1 die (6), 2 dice (36), 1 coin (2), n coins (2^n).
5. Know the 52-card deck composition thoroughly.
