# 8.13 Boats and Streams

## Overview

Boats and Streams is a direct application of Speed, Distance, and Time with the added
concept of a medium (water) that assists or resists motion. The same principles apply
to any scenario involving a moving medium -- escalators, moving walkways, wind affecting
aircraft, or river currents affecting swimmers.

## What You Will Learn

- Speed in still water vs effective speed in a stream
- Upstream and downstream concepts
- Formulas for finding boat speed and stream speed
- Time comparisons for upstream vs downstream travel
- Round trip problems and average speed on rivers
- Multiple boat / meeting-in-river problems
