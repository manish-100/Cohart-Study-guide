### A22. (c) 5

```
2nC3 / nC3 = 12
[2n(2n-1)(2n-2)/6] / [n(n-1)(n-2)/6] = 12
2n(2n-1)(2n-2) / [n(n-1)(n-2)] = 12

Simplify: 2n = 2n, (2n-2) = 2(n-1)
= [2n x (2n-1) x 2(n-1)] / [n x (n-1) x (n-2)]
= [4n(n-1)(2n-1)] / [n(n-1)(n-2)]
= 4(2n-1) / (n-2) = 12

4(2n-1) = 12(n-2)
8n - 4 = 12n - 24
20 = 4n
n = 5
```
