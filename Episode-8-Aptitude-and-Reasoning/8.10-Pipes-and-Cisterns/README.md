# 8.10 Pipes and Cisterns

## Overview

**Pipes and Cisterns** is a direct extension of Work and Time problems. Instead of workers completing a job, we have pipes filling or emptying a tank (cistern). An **inlet pipe** fills the tank (positive work), while an **outlet pipe** or **leak** empties it (negative work). Problems involve finding how long it takes to fill or empty a cistern when multiple pipes and leaks operate together.

This topic appears frequently in competitive exams (SSC, Banking, CAT, GATE, placement tests) because it combines rate concepts with practical scenarios. Once you master Work and Time (8.9), pipes and cisterns problems become straightforward.

## One-Line Formula Cheat Sheet

```
If a pipe fills a tank in X hours, rate of filling     = 1/X per hour
If a pipe empties a tank in Y hours, rate of emptying  = 1/Y per hour
Net rate (inlet + outlet)                              = 1/X - 1/Y
Time to fill with net rate                             = 1 / (Net Rate)
Two inlets (X, Y hrs)                                  = XY / (X + Y) hours
Inlet (X hrs) + Outlet (Y hrs), X < Y                 = XY / (Y - X) hours
Pipe fills in X hrs, leak empties in Y hrs (Y > X)     = XY / (Y - X) hours
Three pipes: net rate                                  = 1/A + 1/B - 1/C
Capacity of cistern (LCM method)                       = LCM(all pipe times)
```
