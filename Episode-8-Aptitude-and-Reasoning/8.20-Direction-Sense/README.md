# 8.20 Direction Sense

## Overview

Direction Sense is a critical topic in Logical Reasoning that tests your ability to determine directions, distances, and positions based on movement descriptions. These problems appear frequently in competitive exams like SSC, Banking, UPSC, CAT, and placement aptitude tests.

## What You Will Learn

1. **Cardinal & Intercardinal Directions** -- N, S, E, W, NE, NW, SE, SW
2. **Left/Right Turning Rules** -- How turns change your facing direction
3. **Distance and Displacement** -- Total path vs. shortest distance
4. **Shadow-Based Direction Problems** -- Using sunrise/sunset to find facing direction
5. **Pythagoras Theorem Application** -- Finding shortest distance between two points
